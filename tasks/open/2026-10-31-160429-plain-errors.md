# Plain errors

A refactor with no change in behavior: the round client and the plan check throw
plain errors (`architecture.md`, Requests, Plan runner).

- `RoundError` and `BadPlan` are removed; their places throw `Error` with the
  same messages. Nothing tells them apart from other errors.
- The round client's errors carry no `cause`; nothing reads it.
- The tests check the messages instead of the classes.
