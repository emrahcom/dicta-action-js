# Page load marker

Check for a page load only after a call of a capability with `pageLoad`, as
`specs/architecture.md` (Plan runner, Page loads) and `contract-capability.md`
(Page changes) in `dicta-action-server` describe.

- A `navigate` event during or after a call of any other capability is ignored,
  also when a later call has `pageLoad` and starts no page load.
- The test module's capabilities that load a page get `pageLoad: true`.
- Unit test: the page load check runs only after calls of capabilities with
  `pageLoad`, also in a `forEach`.
- Browser tests, in all three engines:
  - a capability without `pageLoad` saves a Blob with a `download` link, and the
    next step runs;
  - a download in a step without `pageLoad`, then a step with `pageLoad` that
    loads no page: the plan goes on;
  - a capability without `pageLoad` that loads a page ends the request as
    cancelled at that step.
