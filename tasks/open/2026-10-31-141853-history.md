# History

The element keeps finished requests, and the built-in capabilities read them, as
`architecture.md` (Storage, Built-in capabilities) and `contract-capability.md`
in `dicta-action-server` (Built-in capabilities) and `contract-protocol.md`
(Added text and cancel) describe. Page changes come in their own task.

- When a request ends, the element stores it once in `dicta-action-history`: its
  `requestId`, `time` (when it ended), `text`, `outcome` and `exchange`. The
  outcome is `done`, `stop` or `info` with the server's text, `cancelled` or
  `error`. A request that ended without a `requestId` is not stored.
- A request cancelled while a plan ran gets its last report with the halt code
  `cancelled` at the step or item where it stopped, after the running step
  finished.
- Newest first, at most 20 requests; when storage is full, the oldest go first.
  Storage that cannot be used does not break a request.
- `dictaCapabilities`, exported from the package, with `dicta.listHistory` and
  `dicta.getHistory`, their `args` and `output` schemas, levels and
  descriptions, reading the history from session storage themselves: `limit`
  from 1, 10 by default, texts cut to 200 characters; a request in full by
  `requestId`, `not_found` when it is not kept, with the outputs of its reports
  left out into `tooLarge` when it is over 16 KB.
- Unit tests for the built-in capabilities and browser tests for what is stored.
