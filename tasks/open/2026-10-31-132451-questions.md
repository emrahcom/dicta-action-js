# Questions

A request goes on over several rounds, and a question is asked and answered, as
`architecture.md` (Requests, Display) and `contract-protocol.md` in
`dicta-action-server` (Request body, Response body) describe.

- The request keeps its `requestId` from the first reply and its exchange, and
  sends them in every later round.
- A question shows the `question` view: its text, its options as buttons and
  Cancel. The status region announces the text.
- While a question is open, `submit(text)` answers it, and so does a click on an
  option, with the option's text. The question and its answer go into the
  exchange as one item, and the next round is sent with `working` shown again.
- Cancel while a question is open ends the request.
- Browser tests for each point, with more than one round.
