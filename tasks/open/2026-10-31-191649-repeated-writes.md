# Repeated writes

Top-level steps that call the same `write` capability ask once when there are
more of them than `confirmOver`, as `contract-plan.md` in `dicta-action-server`
(Confirmation) and `architecture.md` (Display, Strings) describe.

- The runner counts the top-level steps of each `write` capability; calls in a
  `forEach` are not counted. Without `confirmOver` in the module, it is 10.
- When a capability has more than `confirmOver` of them, the runner asks before
  the first of them runs, with its filled-in arguments: that step's `confirm`
  text and the number of these steps, in the `items` string.
- Continue runs all of them without asking again. Cancel halts at that step with
  `cancelled`, and it does not run.
- With `confirmOver` or fewer, they do not ask. `destructive` and `external`
  steps ask as before.
- Unit tests: more than `confirmOver` steps ask once with the first text and the
  count; exactly `confirmOver` do not ask; steps of different capabilities are
  counted apart; `forEach` calls are not counted; Cancel halts at the first.
- A browser test: the confirmation view with the text and the `items` string.
