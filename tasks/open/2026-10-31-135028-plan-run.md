# Plan run

Run a checked plan with the module's capabilities and build its report, as
`architecture.md` (Plan runner), `contract-plan.md` (Steps, Include output,
References, Failures, Limits) and `contract-protocol.md` (Report) in
`dicta-action-server` describe. Confirmations, added text, cancel and page
changes come in their own tasks.

- A function that takes a plan that passed the check and the module, runs the
  steps in order and returns the report.
- References: `$1`, `$item` and paths into them, filled in with their JSON type;
  `$$` passed on as a single `$`. Each call's arguments are checked against the
  schema again once filled in.
- `forEach` runs its `do` calls once for each item of an array, at most 100
  items; its output is an array with the outputs of its calls for each item.
- Halts at the first failure, with its code and message: `capability` or the
  capability's own `code`, `reference` and `limit`. Messages are cut to 200
  characters. In a `forEach` after its first item started, `item` and `do`, and
  the `halt.outputs` of the items that ran.
- Included outputs, in step order with `halt.outputs` last, are at most 16 KB
  together as the UTF-8 bytes of their JSON; one that does not fit goes into
  `tooLarge` or `halt.tooLarge` with its size.
- Unit tests for each point.
