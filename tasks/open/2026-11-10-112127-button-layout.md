# Button layout

The buttons of each view and notice go under the rest of it, in a box with the
`buttons` part, aligned to the left, as `architecture.md` (Element, Display)
says. Today a view or a notice is one wrapping row, so its buttons flow after
the text like words.

- `question`: the options, one per line, then Cancel on a line of its own.
- `confirmation`: Continue and Cancel side by side on one line.
- `working` and a notice: their button on a line of its own.

The element's CSS changes with it: a view and a notice stack their parts; in
`working`, the indicator and the label stay on one line. The texts of a
confirmation stack one under another.

Test: each view and a notice have their buttons in the `buttons` part, after the
text; in a browser test, the options of a question are one under another,
Continue and Cancel of a confirmation are side by side, and the buttons start at
the left edge of the text.
