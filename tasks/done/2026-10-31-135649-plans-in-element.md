# Plans in the element

The element runs the plans the server sends and reports back, as
`architecture.md` (Display, Plan runner) and `contract-plan.md` (Label,
Reporting, Failures) and `contract-protocol.md` (Request body, Report) in
`dicta-action-server` describe. Confirmations come in their own task.

- A plan reply is checked against the site context. A plan that fails gets a
  report with the halt code `invalid` and the reason, without `step`; no step
  runs.
- A plan that passes runs with the plan runner, while the `working` view shows
  its label, when it has one. The status region announces the label.
- The plan, unchanged, and its report go into the exchange as one item, and the
  next round is sent, whether the plan finished or halted.
- Until confirmations come, a step that needs one runs without asking.
- Browser tests: a plan that runs and reports its outputs, a halted plan, an
  invalid plan, the label shown and announced, and a request with a plan, a
  question and done.
