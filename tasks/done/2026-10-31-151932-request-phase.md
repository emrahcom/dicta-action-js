# Request phase

A refactor of the element: the request's phase is one field instead of several
flags. The behavior does not change (`architecture.md`, Requests, Display).

- `phase` on the running request: `waiting` while a round is sent, `question`
  while a question is open, `running` while a plan runs and `confirmation` while
  a confirmation is open; Continue goes back to `running`.
- It replaces the `confirming` and `running` flags, and the question's text as a
  sign of an open question; the text stays as data for the answer. `plan` and
  `progress` stay as data of the running plan; `added` and `left` stay as they
  are.
- Every rule reads the phase: what `submit` does, what Cancel and `pagehide` do.
- The tests pass unchanged.
